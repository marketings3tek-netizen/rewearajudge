Legal Governance & Protection Policies

Effective: Academic Year 2026–2027 • Reweara Circular Systems Pvt Ltd (Chennai, Tamil Nadu)

1. Campus Privacy Policy

Reweara collects minimal student data required to facilitate safe peer-to-peer wardrobe sharing across Chennai colleges (Madras Christian College, Guru Nanak College, IIT Madras, VIT Chennai, Stella Maris, Loyola, and SRM).

A. Information We Collect:

  • Student Verification: Full name, institutional college email address, student roll/identity card number.
  • Financial Settlement: Encrypted UPI VPA address for 60% lender earnings distribution. No debit card credentials or PINs are stored on our servers.
  • Logistics Data: Campus locker pickup timestamps and QR authentication scans.

B. Non-Disclosure & Storage:

Student phone numbers are never publicly disclosed to peers. All counter-offers, negotiations, and fitting queries take place securely within our in-app negotiation channel. Data is hosted on ISO 27001-certified Indian data center servers in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act.

2. Terms of Campus Service

By renting, subscribing, or listing clothes on Reweara, you agree to abide by our circular campus etiquette and timely locker return guidelines.

A. Booking & Campus Drop Protocol:

  • Rentals are active for 24-hour cycles starting at locker pickup. Extensions must be booked 6 hours before scheduled return.
  • Garments must be returned to the designated college ambassador locker in the supplied CleanCheck™ garment sleeve.
  • Students must not attempt aggressive DIY spot removals with bleach or boiling water; our CleanCheck™ facility treats delicate fabrics professionally.

B. Subscription Rules (Reweara Rotate Pass):

The Casual Pass (₹349/month) and Campus Icon Pass (₹599/month) auto-renew monthly on UPI Autopay. Students can pause or cancel at any time with one tap before the next billing cycle. No lock-in contracts.

3. Reweara Shield™ Zero-Deposit & Refund Policy

Traditional rental platforms lock up ₹500 to ₹2,000 security deposits. Reweara utilizes a community-backed 5% micro-insurance pool so students can dress up without upfront financial burdens.

A. Zero Security Deposit Guarantee:

Verified students do not pay security deposits. Accidental food spills, caught hems, or stuck zippers are repaired using our 5% Shield Protection Pool without renter penalties, provided the student files a report within 12 hours of the incident.

B. Cancellation & Instant Refund Timeline:

  • Pre-Pickup Cancellation: Full 100% refund credited immediately to your UPI if canceled before locker packing.
  • Fit Discrepancy / Size Issue: If a garment does not fit upon locker pickup, return it within 2 hours to receive an immediate 100% rental credit voucher.
  • Lender Payout Timeline: 60% earnings are disbursed automatically to the lender's UPI account within 4 hours of the garment returning to the campus locker.
